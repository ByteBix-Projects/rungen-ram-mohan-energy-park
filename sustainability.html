Circular Economy

Waste-to-Energy and Agriculture Integration

Rather than consuming resource-heavy fossil sources, RUNGEN RAMMOHAN ENERGY PARK operates entirely on a circular feed model. We convert regional crop residues, bagasse, and municipal waste into clean molecules.

This prevents open-air crop stubble burning—a major cause of air pollution across India—while providing local farmers with a secondary source of income for their agricultural residues.

Resource Efficiency Priority

Our catalysts are optimized to operate at high selectivity, converting up to 92% of organic carbon feedstock into usable transport and industrial energy fractions.

Social Welfare in Srikakulam

Guided by our Director, Mr. Pedada Ramamohanarao, the founders of RUNGEN RAMMOHAN ENERGY PARK believe that industrial success must directly uplift surrounding communities.

For over two decades, Mr. Ramamohanarao has directed business profits into public-service initiatives:

  • 📚 Education: Financing tuition fees and books for underprivileged regional students.
  • 🏥 Healthcare: Organizing medical checkup events, blood drives, and ambulance supports.
  • 🏘️ Rehabilitation: Rebuilding housing and public infrastructure during coastal cyclonic weather events.
  • 📖 Libraries: Constructing rural reading centers to improve literacy rates.
National Priority Alignment

Supporting India’s Green Mandates

Our clean-energy park aligns with national decarbonization programs promoted by the Government of India:

• Ethanol Blending Programme (EBP): Providing 1G/2G ethanol fractions to assist OMCs in hitting the national 20% petrol blending targets.
• National Green Hydrogen Mission: Constructing water-electrolysis structures to build decentralized green hydrogen supplies.
• Net-Zero 2070 Roadmap: Deploying carbon capturing and Ranjan Zeo Catalyst waste processing to create domestic sustainable aviation fuels (SAF).
