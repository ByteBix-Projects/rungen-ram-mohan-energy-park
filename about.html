About Our Company

Establishing India's leading integrated clean energy park powered by scientific innovation and circular principles.

Who We Are

RUNGEN RAMMOHAN ENERGY PARK PRIVATE LIMITED is an advanced energy company focused on manufacturing next-generation clean fuels and innovative power solutions. The company’s core activities span the production of CNG, Bio-Methanol, Ethanol, Biofuel, Green Hydrogen, CO₂-based fuels, and Sustainable Aviation Fuel (SAF), positioning it as an integrated energy producer.

The company aims to address India’s fast-growing demand for clean and affordable energy, leveraging cutting-edge research, proprietary catalytic technologies, and deep industrial experience across multiple process industries.

We prioritize high-efficiency, low-emission fuel manufacturing, with a strong emphasis on circular economy principles, utilizing waste and agricultural biomass as feedstock to reduce environmental impact.

Why We Were Founded

"India is moving fast towards its net-zero carbon goals. We saw an urgent need to build a technology-backed energy park that does not rely on simple fossil fuel processes, but instead takes waste material and industrial CO₂ and converts them back into usable energy streams."

- DR. D.K. RAGARANJAN, CEO
👁️

Our Vision

To become a leading integrated clean-energy park in India, producing environmentally sustainable fuels and power solutions that reduce carbon emissions, enhance energy security, and support national net-zero goals.

🚀

Our Mission

• Develop and operate advanced plants for CNG, Bio-Methanol, Ethanol, Biofuels, Green Hydrogen, and SAF at commercially viable scale.

• Convert waste and biomass into value-added energy products, using proprietary catalytic and process technologies.

• Deliver reliable, high-quality fuel to industrial, transport, aviation, and commercial customers while maintaining strict safety standards.
