Our Partnership Models

Working closely with landowners, industrial companies, and energy distributors.

Commercial & Operational Collaboration

1. Long-Term Off-Take Contracts

Secure stable volume agreements for green hydrogen, bio-CNG, and ethanol blending to de-risk corporate sustainability planning.

2. Biomass Feedstock Supply Chains

Partnering with regional agricultural cooperatives and municipal waste managers for steady feedstock input flows.

3. Industrial Integration (CO₂ Capture)

Installing carbon capture modules directly onto industrial flue lines to synthesize fuels on-site using our catalytic patent.

Co-Developing Energy Parks

We collaborate with state government bodies, utility providers, and infrastructure developers to construct regional multi-product energy park units.

Our engineering team offers full execution support—covering front-end design, environmental compliance, catalyst supplies, and asset valuation.
