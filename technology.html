Proprietary Technology

Ranjan’s Zeo Catalyst & Waste Treatment

The operational foundation of RUNGEN RAMMOHAN ENERGY PARK is built upon the Ph.D. research of our Chief Executive Officer, Dr. D.K. Raga Ranjan Reddy. His doctoral thesis, titled "Energy from systematic treatment of waste and the role of Ranjan’s Zeo catalyst" (Yogi Vemana University), directly informs the park's biological and chemical conversion pathways.

Traditional waste-to-energy processes suffer from low conversion efficiencies and toxic emissions. The Ranjan Zeo Catalyst resolves this by offering:

  • 🔑 High selectivity: Directing the thermal cracking of biomass molecules specifically into short-chain clean fuels rather than complex tars.
  • ⚡ Lower activation temperature: Reducing the electrical energy input required to operate refinery boilers and gasifiers.
  • 🛡️ Toxin inhibition: Restricting the synthesis of heavy aromatic pollutants during municipal waste gasification.
  • 🌾 Biomass to value-add products: Upgrading organic residue fractions into premium value-added materials.
  • 🚢 Export-oriented bio products: Manufacturing next-generation clean fuels meeting strict international export standards.
