Clean Transport

Compressed Natural Gas (CNG) & Bio-CNG

The company plans to produce CNG and Bio-CNG from organic waste feedstocks, replacing conventional fossil-fuel based CNG with lower-carbon alternatives.

Our Bio-CNG production leverages anaerobic digestion and advanced catalytic upgrading to convert raw biogas into pipeline-grade gas suitable for transport and industrial use.

Key Applications:

  • 🚗 Automotive fuel for clean-energy CNG vehicles
  • 🏭 Industrial and commercial heating systems
  • 🌐 Blending into city gas distribution networks

Environmental Impact

Bio-CNG offers up to an 80% reduction in net greenhouse gas emissions compared to diesel and petrol, helping municipalities utilize local waste and clean up city air.

Status: Under engineering phase

Renewable Feedstock Origin

By recycling carbon from organic agricultural residues, we create a circular molecule that prevents waste burning in open fields while building chemical sovereignty for India.

Status: Catalytic process design complete
Green Chemical

Bio-Methanol

RUNGEN RAMMOHAN ENERGY PARK will manufacture Bio-Methanol, a renewable methanol produced from biomass, agricultural residues, or waste-derived syngas using specialized catalytic processes.

Bio-Methanol is highly versatile. It can be used as a liquid fuel, a high-octane blending component for transportation, a feedstock for green chemical production, and as an efficient hydrogen carrier in fuel cell systems.

Key Applications:

  • 🚢 Marine fuel for low-emission international shipping
  • 🏭 Raw material for green solvents and plastics
  • ⚡ Hydrogen carrier for decentralized power cells
Bio-Refinery Core

Ethanol & Advanced Biofuels

The company's product portfolio includes 1G (First Generation) and 2G (Second Generation) ethanol, produced from sugar-based inputs and lignocellulosic biomass feedstocks respectively.

Process development in high-concentration ethanol fermentation and efficient enzyme hydrolysis are major technological priorities led by our scientific leadership, maximizing conversion rates.

Key Applications:

  • ⛽ Petrol blending in compliance with India's national mandate
  • 💊 Specialty chemical and pharmaceutical grade alcohol
  • 🍃 Feedstock for green industrial solvent manufacturing

1G vs 2G Biofuels

While 1G ethanol utilizes starch and food crops, our 2G research is fully focused on agricultural residues (rice straw, sugarcane bagasse), preventing food-versus-fuel conflict.

R&D Lead: Dr. D.K. Ragaranjan
Decarbonizing Aviation

Sustainable Aviation Fuel (SAF)

We aim to manufacture Sustainable Aviation Fuel (SAF) to support deep decarbonization in commercial aviation and heavy transport sectors.

Our SAF production plans utilize catalytic hydro-processing of waste lipids and synthesize intermediates from synthesis gas into jet-fuel compliant hydrocarbon fractions.

Key Applications:

  • ✈️ Drop-in fuel for commercial passenger and cargo aircraft
  • 📦 Green shipping options for international airlines
  • 🛠️ Meets ASTM D7566 aviation specifications
Zero Emission

Green Hydrogen

RUNGEN RAMMOHAN ENERGY PARK plans to develop Green Hydrogen production capacities using water electrolysis powered by renewable electricity.

Our green hydrogen pathways will be integrated directly with bio-processes and catalytic CO₂ systems. The produced hydrogen can be blended with natural gas or used as a clean chemical reduction agent.

Key Applications:

  • 🏭 Decarbonizing steel, fertilizer, and cement plants
  • 🚌 Clean fuel cell buses and heavy-duty logistics
  • 🔥 Natural gas grid injection for cleaner combustion

Climate Technology

CO₂-Based Fuels & Carbon-Sink Technologies

A key differentiator for our park is our focus on CO₂ utilization, driven by Dr. Ragaranjan's research and patent on "carbon sink and fuels from carbon dioxide."

Rather than releasing industrial CO₂ into the atmosphere, we capture flue gases from industrial plants or bio-refineries. By combining this CO₂ with hydrogen over specialized catalysts, we convert it back into methanol, methane, or synthetic paraffins.

Key Applications:

  • 💨 Direct carbon capture from steel, cement, or power plants
  • 🔄 Production of carbon-neutral synthetic diesel and kerosene
  • 🌱 Long-term solid carbon storage and sink creation
